### Hi, I'm Chengkai

I started out in aerospace with a BEng at Manchester, before realising I was more interested in the systems moving the money than the things flying through the air. So I trained as an auditor, qualified as an ACA, and spent four years auditing retailers in London.

The job taught me something quickly: most professional finance work is just moving data between spreadsheets and ERPs. I started automating bits of it with Python, SQL and Power BI, and the more I automated, the more I wanted to go further.

That's what brought me back to school. I'm now doing an MSc in Computer Science at QMUL, and my dissertation is an AI agent that lives inside Excel — the tool I wish I'd had during those four years of audit. Built with FastAPI, Gemini, and Office.js.

Outside of all that, I cook a fair bit. Slowly working out what I want to build next.
